# Efika Axiom — the three-persona demo script (P5, v1)

**Status: DRAFT v1 · 2026-08-15** — the Slick Program's P5 deliverable
(`docs/plan/platform-slick-program.md` §5-P5, amendment 4). This is the acceptance instrument:
the program is done when this script walks start-to-finish with zero credibility defects and
zero dead controls.

**Relationship to the existing choreography.** `docs/platform/08-demo-choreography.md` remains
valid and is **not contradicted** — it choreographs a *different* product surface: the onboarding
machine and Control Plane on **:8420** (Acts 0–10, DemoBank, the Agent CLI, the publish refusal).
This script choreographs **the platform itself on :8400** for three banker personas. Where the two
overlap (mapping, data quality, reconciliation, governance) the platform's own screens are used
here, and the :8420 machine story becomes an **optional appendix** (§10) for a technical audience
that asks "how did the data get in?".

**Register (the presentation culture — binding).** Formal, credential-led, evidenced. No flashy
opener, no magic trick, no countdown. Capability impresses **inside a structured chapter**: state
what the screen is, state the number, state where the number comes from, move on. The single
ratified exception is **AI capability**, which the charter foregrounds (amendment 5) — there,
enthusiasm is licensed and on-brand.

**The evidence rule.** Every figure spoken aloud carries its **as-of**. If the screen and this
document disagree, **read the screen** — this document is a rehearsal aid, not a source of truth.
Never quote a number this script states without seeing it on the screen first.

## 3. Chapter CFO — the close, the statements, the plan (≤15 minutes)

**Audience seat:** the Chief Financial Officer and the Chief Accountant.
**Claim of the chapter:** the close is evidenced end-to-end — every statement figure opens into
the working paper that produced it, and the budget sits on the same actuals.

### CFO-1 · The IFRS cockpit (2 min)
- **Route:** `/ifrs/dashboard`
- **Do:** land, let the headline figures settle. Read the as-of line under the title.
- **Say:** "This is the IFRS 9 position as at the date on the header. Expected credit loss by
  stage, coverage, and the movement since the prior close. Nothing here is keyed by hand —
  it is the output of the conversion I am about to show you."
- ★ **Signature (PENDING P4):** click a headline figure → it drills to the population behind it.
  Until the P4 drill wiring lands, do not gesture at the KPI as if it were clickable.
- **Risk:** engine unreachable → the cockpit shows a visible error card with a retry (built in
  P1). **Recovery — and this is itself a demo point:** "The platform will not show you a stale
  number when it cannot reach the engine. It tells you, and it offers a retry." Click retry once.
  If it fails twice, move to CFO-2 and return later.

### CFO-2 · Conversion evidence ★ (4 min)
- **Route:** `/ifrs/conversion` → open **conversion 356** (`/ifrs/conversion/356`)
- **Do:** show the conversion ledger first (the closed-period set: FY-2024, Q1-2025, H1-2025,
  Q3-2025, Oct-2025, Nov-2025 — conversions 171, 172, 173, 174, 177, 178 — plus the current
  working conversion). Open 356. Walk the step list, stop on the data-quality gate.
- **Say:** "A conversion is not a button, it is a sequence with a gate. Thirty-three checks in
  three categories — completeness, accuracy, validity. Ten of those are model checks that run at
  the end. For this conversion: 33 of 33 pass, zero failures, and the four currency ties agree to
  the cent — difference zero point zero zero. As-of 31 December 2025."
- **Say (the timing, plainly, no theatre):** "The full run is under four minutes on a book of
  7.1 million rows. Two sessions ago it was 11.4 minutes; the difference is a classification
  step that now runs once at book refresh instead of once per conversion."
- ★ **Signature moment (≤30 s):** open the gate panel and point at the four currency ties reading
  0.00. "This is what 'it ties' means here — not approximately, to the cent, per currency."
- **Risk:** a live run is slow or a step spins. **Recovery:** do not run a live conversion unless
  it was rehearsed warm today. Show 356's stored results instead — they are the same evidence.
  If asked "can you run one now?", answer: "Yes — it takes just under four minutes; I will start
  it now and we will come back to it at the end of this chapter." Then actually come back.

### CFO-3 · Statements + working-paper trace ★ (4 min)
- **Route:** `/ifrs/statements`
- **Do:** open the statement set. Find a figure carrying a **trace chip**. Click it → the working
  paper opens (`/ifrs/wp/<code>`).
- **Say:** "This is the statement as it will be filed. Every figure that has a working paper
  behind it carries a chip. I click the chip —" (click) "— and this is the paper: the population,
  the calculation, the totals row that foots. The figure on the statement and the total on this
  paper are the same number, from the same run."
- **Say (the as-of, every time):** "As-of 31 December 2025, conversion 356."
- ★ **Signature moment (≤30 s):** statement figure → chip → working paper → the totals row.
  No narration needed beyond "same number, one click".
- **Risk:** the chip does not render on the figure the presenter picked. **Recovery:** rehearse
  **one specific figure** and use only that one. Note the exact statement line in the cue card
  (§11) after the dry run. If chips are absent entirely, the P4/WP wiring did not land — fall back
  to `/ifrs/report/pack` and speak the trace as a roadmap item honestly.
- **Verify in the dry run:** `WpTraceChip` is imported by both `IfrsStatements.tsx` and
  `StatementPresentation.tsx`; the rendering path has not been walked by a human yet.

### CFO-4 · The Plan cockpit (2 min)
- **Route:** `/plan`
- **Do:** land on the verdict strip. State the as-of.
- **Say:** "The budget is not a spreadsheet on someone's laptop. It is built on the same canonical
  actuals you just saw, with a four-eyes approval on every submitted version."
- **Risk:** one of the two parallel fetches behind the verdict strip fails and it used to spin
  forever — **fixed in P1**: it now times out and shows an error with a retry. If it appears,
  use the same honesty line as CFO-1.
- **Note:** the Plan module is on the universal kit since the Part 4 pass (06-Sep-2026) and the
  lane P wave-1 polish (14-Sep-2026); the five-minute Plan path is §13 below.

### CFO-5 · Breakback ★ (2 min)
- **Route:** `/plan/builder`
- **Do:** open a total, change it, and distribute the change down by driver.
- **Say:** "Planning happens top-down and bottom-up at once. I take the total the board gave me,
  I change it here, and the platform spreads it down the driver tree — proportionally, or by the
  driver I choose. Every leaf below moves, and the total still ties."
- ★ **Signature moment (≤30 s):** type a new total → the leaves redistribute → the footer total
  agrees. Silence works better than commentary here.
- **Status: LIVE** — "Distribute a target" on `/plan/builder` (the disclosure above the ledger):
  type the target NET, preview the driver moves, apply. **Precondition: the cycle is in draft** —
  drivers lock once it is submitted; send it back from `/plan/approvals` first (§13).

### CFO-6 · Plan versus actual (1 min)
- **Route:** `/plan/analysis`
- **Say:** "And this is the same plan against the same actuals — one source, so the variance is
  arithmetic, not an argument about which extract is right. As-of the period on the header."
- **Close the chapter:** "That is the close: an evidenced conversion, statements that open into
  their working papers, and a budget standing on the same numbers."

- **Route:** `/data/dq-rules`

- **Say:** "Rules are data, not code. Before I publish a change I test it against history — this

- **Do:** publish through the four-eyes flow (this is a governed change — it goes to the approver).
- ★ **Signature moment (≤30 s) — ROLLBACK:** open the same rule and click rollback. "And this is

  the same approval as the change did."
- **Say if the rule has no history:** the screen says so itself — "This rule has never been changed
  through the approval flow, so there is no earlier version to restore." Read it aloud: "It tells

- **Risk:** the backtest is slow on a large scope. **Recovery:** rehearse one rule with a bounded
